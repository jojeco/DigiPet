This is a DigiPet app, Like a tamagotchi but not because of copyright

Press on the dog to pat him and his happiness goes up (+1 point), long press and it goes up even more (+5 points). When his happiness is above 50 a treat will come back after a while — using it earns another 2 points and makes him even happier. He will do a little jump when he is played with and bark too, even your phone will vibrate. Points earned from patting, long-pressing, and using treats are saved between launches so your score carries over next time you open the app.
